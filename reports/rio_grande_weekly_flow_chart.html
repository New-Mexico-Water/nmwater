Rio Grande weekly flow

Average streamflow in cubic feet per second (cfs) for each New Mexico segment of the river, by week, from Sep 22, 2025 to Sep 20, 2026. A segment is the watershed (HUC8) its gauges sit in. Each segment line is the mean of its gauges' weekly means.

Weekly mean flow by segment, upstream to downstream

Hover, touch or use the arrow keys to read a week. Click a segment name to hide or show it. Every value is also in the summary and the weekly table.

    Values are means of daily means, cfs. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

    Segment summary

    Year mean is the average of the weekly segment values that were reported.

    How to read this

    • A segment value averages gauges at different points on the river. Diversions, returns and tributary inflow between gauges move it, so it is not the flow at any one place. A segment with one gauge is that gauge.
    • Each physical gauge is published by several agencies (USGS, USBR, Corps of Engineers, Colorado DWR). Copies are merged so a gauge counts once, and the USGS daily mean is used first.
    • A gauge counts in a segment when it reported at least 26 of the 52 weeks. Gauges with shorter records are left out of the segment line.
    • Recent values are provisional and can be revised. The week in progress is not shown.

    Source: New Mexico water data archive, weeks through Sep 20, 2026. Method, gauge list and copy-agreement checks are in docs/reports/rio-grande-weekly-flow.md.
