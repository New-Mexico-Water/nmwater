    How to read this

      __EXTRA_NOTES__
    • A segment value averages gauges at different points on the river. Diversions, returns and tributary inflow between gauges move it, so it is not the flow at any one place.
    • The gauges behind a segment change over the decades as gauges open and close. The hover readout shows how many were reporting; a jump in that count can move a line without any change in the river.
    • Each physical gauge is published by several agencies (USGS, USBR, Corps of Engineers, Colorado DWR). Copies are merged so a gauge counts once, and the USGS daily mean is used first.
    • Weekly: mean of daily means, Monday to Sunday, at least 4 days. Monthly: mean of the weeks starting in that month, at least 3. Yearly: mean of the weeks starting in that year, at least 45, so the current partial year is left out of yearly views. A gauge counts in a segment when it reported at least __MIN_WEEKS__ weeks in the whole record.
    • Recent values are provisional and can be revised. The week in progress is not shown.
