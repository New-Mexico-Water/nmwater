# Costilla Reservoir

_Generated by `scripts/reservoir_fill.py` from `catalog/reservoirs.yaml`. Edit the registry, not this file._

A privately owned irrigation reservoir in the Sangre de Cristo high country, built in 1920 and operated by the Rio Costilla Cooperative Livestock Association. Its water is shared with Colorado under the Costilla Creek Compact.
