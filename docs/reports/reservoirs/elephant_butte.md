# Elephant Butte Reservoir

_Generated by `scripts/reservoir_fill.py` from `catalog/reservoirs.yaml`. Edit the registry, not this file._

New Mexico's largest reservoir and the storage for Reclamation's Rio Grande Project, which irrigates the Elephant Butte Irrigation District in New Mexico and El Paso County Water Improvement District No. 1 in Texas and supplies Mexico's treaty delivery. It is also where New Mexico's Rio Grande Compact deliveries to Texas are measured.

## How to use this data

- **The lower Rio Grande's water supply.** Storage going into the irrigation season sets the Project allocation.
- **Compact accounting.** Reservoir storage and releases are the basis of New Mexico's delivery obligation to Texas. Low storage also triggers Compact Article VII limits on upstream storage.
- **Long-term sediment.** The 112-year record documents the loss of about a quarter of the original reservoir to sediment. See elephant-butte-fill.md for the curated treatment.

## Read before quoting a number

- Elephant Butte has a dedicated, hand-reviewed report (scripts/elephant_butte_fill.py). This general report reproduces its capacities using the same 14 table-adoption dates.
- Evaporation from the reservoir is large, often a hundred thousand acre-feet or more a year, and is reported separately by Reclamation.
