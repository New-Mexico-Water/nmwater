## How to use this data

- **Irrigation supply for the middle valley.** El Vado storage going into the irrigation season is the best single indicator of how much native water MRGCD has to work with.
- **Rio Grande Compact constraints.** Storage in post-1929 reservoirs above Otowi is limited when Elephant Butte is low under Article VII of the Rio Grande Compact, so El Vado's ability to store in a given spring depends on conditions downstream.
- **Chama runoff timing.** Inflow is a direct record of spring snowmelt from the Chama headwaters.

## Read before quoting a number

- Storage has been heavily restricted in recent years during dam safety work. The near-empty years of 2022 to 2024 reflect that, not drought alone. Confirm the restriction dates with Reclamation before attributing them.
- An earlier version of this report used Reclamation's 2007 survey table and needed an unexplained 1.45 ft datum shift. The operator's current table, effective 2021, reproduces reported storage exactly with no shift, which resolves that discrepancy.
