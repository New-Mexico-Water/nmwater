## How to use this data

- **Carlsbad Project supply.** With Brantley and Santa Rosa, Sumner holds the Pecos irrigation supply.
- **Block releases.** Release records show the large block releases used to move water down the Pecos efficiently.

## Read before quoting a number

- This is a flood-control dam. Percent full is measured against the conservation pool, which is what the dam normally holds. Storage above it is flood water being held back, measured by `days_flood_storage` and `peak_flood_pct`. Dividing storage by the dam's total or design capacity answers a different question and will read very low.
- The top of the active conservation pool was lowered in June 2012 for dam maintenance, to 4,260.9 ft. The earlier, higher pool is not published in CWMS, so every year is measured against the reduced pool. Before 2012 that overstates percent full, and it counts as flood storage water that was then ordinary conservation storage. Percentages above 100% before 2012 should not be read as floods.
- Sumner has a flood pool above its conservation pool (Flood Risk Reduction is its primary purpose in the dam registry), so it is treated as a flood-control reservoir here.
- Pre-2007 capacities are derived from an elevation and storage pairing that is noisy in this record. Treat individual early years as approximate.
