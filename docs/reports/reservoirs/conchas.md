## How to use this data

- **Canadian basin supply.** Conservation-pool storage is the irrigation supply for the Tucumcari area.
- **Plains storm runoff.** Inflow on the Canadian is dominated by summer storms rather than snowmelt, so it complements the Rio Grande reservoirs.

## Read before quoting a number

- This is a flood-control dam. Percent full is measured against the conservation pool, which is what the dam normally holds. Storage above it is flood water being held back, measured by `days_flood_storage` and `peak_flood_pct`. Dividing storage by the dam's total or design capacity answers a different question and will read very low.
- Before 2004 the record is storage only, with no elevation, so the capacity table in force in those years cannot be identified. Those years use the earliest derived capacity and are flagged.
- No release series is reported here. Most Conchas releases go into the Conchas Canal to Arch Hurley rather than down the Canadian, and the archive's canal and river gauges below the dam stop in the 1970s to 1990s, so they would understate releases badly.

## Capacity history

Reported storage departs from the current table in earlier years because older tables were in force. 2 table eras were found, detected by comparing each year with the previous one over the elevations both occupied. Departure is the median of reported storage minus the current table's storage at the same elevation.
