# Heron Reservoir

_Generated by `scripts/reservoir_fill.py` from `catalog/reservoirs.yaml`. Edit the registry, not this file._

The San Juan-Chama Project's main storage. Water diverted from San Juan River tributaries in Colorado arrives through the Azotea Tunnel and is held here for the Project's contractors, chiefly the Albuquerque Bernalillo County Water Utility Authority, the Middle Rio Grande Conservancy District and the City of Santa Fe. Heron is operated to store imported San Juan-Chama water, not native Rio Grande water.

## How to use this data

- **Imported supply.** Heron storage is the clearest single measure of how much Colorado River basin water New Mexico has banked for the middle Rio Grande. It moves independently of Rio Grande runoff.
- **Contractor calls.** Releases show when contractors call their water down the Chama, typically for municipal supply or to offset river depletions.
- **Snowpack in the San Juan mountains, not the Sangres.** Inflow tracks diversions through the Azotea Tunnel, so a good year here can coincide with a poor year on the Rio Grande.

## Read before quoting a number

- Reclamation's 2010 survey table published in RISE stops at 7,102 ft, far below the operating range. This report uses the complete operator table held in CWMS instead, which reproduces reported storage exactly.
- Full pool is detected from the record, not taken from a published pool definition.

## Capacity history

Reported storage departs from the current table in earlier years because older tables were in force. 2 table eras were found, detected by comparing each year with the previous one over the elevations both occupied. Departure is the median of reported storage minus the current table's storage at the same elevation. Capacities are at today's pool elevations.

Confidence: `current_table` means reported storage reproduces from the current table; `high` means the lake reached the pool elevation during that era, or came within 10 ft; `medium` within 30 ft; `low` farther, or the monotone fit had to move the estimate more than 2%.
