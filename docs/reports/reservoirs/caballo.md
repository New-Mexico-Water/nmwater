# Caballo Reservoir

_Generated by `scripts/reservoir_fill.py` from `catalog/reservoirs.yaml`. Edit the registry, not this file._

Reclamation's re-regulating reservoir 25 miles below Elephant Butte, completed in 1938. It catches Elephant Butte's releases and meters them out to the irrigation diversions below, and it adds flood protection for the lower valley.

## How to use this data

- **Irrigation-season operations.** Caballo fills in winter and early spring from Elephant Butte releases and drains through the irrigation season. Its annual cycle is a clear picture of Rio Grande Project delivery timing.
- **Release to the lower valley.** Caballo release is the Rio Grande Project's delivery to the Rincon and Mesilla valleys and on to Texas.

## Read before quoting a number

- Caballo's storage mostly reflects how Elephant Butte is being operated, not local hydrology. Read the two together.
- The operator table carries a 999999 sentinel above 4,182 ft, which is removed.
