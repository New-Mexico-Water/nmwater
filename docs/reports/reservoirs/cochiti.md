# Cochiti Lake

_Generated by `scripts/reservoir_fill.py` from `catalog/reservoirs.yaml`. Edit the registry, not this file._

The Army Corps' flood and sediment control dam on the main stem of the Rio Grande above Albuquerque, completed in 1975. It keeps a permanent recreation pool of about 1,200 surface acres, maintained with San Juan-Chama water, and otherwise passes native Rio Grande flow through except when it is holding back floods. Everything the Rio Grande delivers to the middle valley passes through Cochiti first.

## How to use this data

- **The middle Rio Grande's inflow gauge.** Cochiti inflow is effectively the whole upper Rio Grande and Chama delivered to the middle valley. Its annual volume and peak are among the most useful single numbers for the health of the system.
- **Flood regulation.** `days_flood_storage` and `peak_flood_pct` show when and how hard the flood pool was used. A spring with the pool filling and then drawing down is snowmelt being metered out.
- **Managed spring releases for the bosque.** The Corps has used deviations in Cochiti's flood operations to hold spring runoff and release it as a shaped pulse that floods the bosque overbank and supports the Rio Grande silvery minnow. Those operations appear here as a spring storage rise followed by a sustained high release. Confirm specific years against Corps deviation records.
- **Sediment trapping.** Cochiti stops most of the upper basin's sediment. Its capacity loss over time is a direct measure of the load that no longer reaches the middle valley and Elephant Butte.

## Read before quoting a number

- This is a flood-control dam. Percent full is measured against the conservation pool, which is what the dam normally holds. Storage above it is flood water being held back, measured by `days_flood_storage` and `peak_flood_pct`. Dividing storage by the dam's total or design capacity answers a different question and will read very low.
- Percent of conservation pool describes the recreation pool only. It is expected to sit near 100% in almost every year and says little about water supply. The flood-pool columns carry the hydrological signal.
- The dam registry's 722,000 acre-foot figure is flood capacity. Do not divide storage by it and call the result how full the lake is.
